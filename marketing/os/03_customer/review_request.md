## 메시지 (English)
Hi [Name]! Thank you so much for studying with me.
I'm building the Step Korean page and would love to share your experience with new learners.
Could you answer 3 short questions? Even 1–2 sentences each is perfect.

1. Before our classes, what was hardest about learning Korean?
2. What changed after our classes?
3. If a friend asked about my class, what would you tell them?

Also, may I use your answer on my website and social media?
Please choose one: (a) first name + photo  (b) first name only  (c) anonymous  (d) please don't share

Thank you! 감사합니다 😊
